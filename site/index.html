spamcha

The invisible antispam for web forms: no captcha, no human tests, no cloud. A signed stamp, three invisible gates, and a judge that reads the content: and learns what your site receives.

The captcha AFTER, not before: bots pay, people just talk.

humano → form → invisible stamp → gates → judge → ✓ inbox
bot    → form → (no stamp | slop)        → judge → ⚠ quarantine / ✗ trash

MIT licensed, zero runtime dependencies, one language (TypeScript) from browser stamp to server judge.

Why

Proof-of-work captchas (ALTCHA, Cap) stop cheap bots but don't read anything. Cloud judges (Akismet, CleanTalk…) read your visitors' words: and ship them to someone else's servers. spamcha judges content on your box: it never phones home, never trains on your data in someone else's cloud, and never makes a human prove they're human.

How it works

render → server signs a stamp (HMAC-SHA256, 30 min TTL) submit → ① stamp valid? ② ≥3 s since render (server clock: unfakeable) ③ honeypot empty? → then the judge reads the text: rules you can edit + a Bayesian learner trained by your "this was garbage" clicks
🔒 StampHMAC-signed render token. A POST without one never rendered your page: rejected with zero spam knowledge.
⏱ Time floorThe issue time lives inside the signature. Three seconds of life or less = not typed by a human.
🍯 HoneypotA hidden field must exist and stay empty. Missing = nobody rendered your HTML.
🧠 JudgeEditable rule pack + per-site Bayesian learner (Graham-style). It reads, explains, and improves from your clicks.

Three verdicts, never a lost customer

pass processed normally · quarantine held for your one-click review: a real person is recovered, not bounced · reject silently dropped. Cold start works from minute zero (rules carry it); every verdict explains itself with immutable codes.

Install

Node site

npm install spamcha
import { createGatekeeper, middlewareExpress } from "spamcha";
const guard = createGatekeeper({ secret, modelPath: "./spamcha.json",
                                 quarantinePath: "./spamcha-quarantine.jsonl",
                                 logPath: "./spamcha-log.jsonl" });
app.post("/contacto", middlewareExpress(guard), handler);
app.get("/panel",     (req, res) => res.send(panelHtml(guard)));

Anything else (PHP, Python, WordPress…): your own local service

spamcha serve --port 8788          # or: docker run spamcha
# your backend: POST http://127.0.0.1:8788/verdict  →  {decision, reasons…}

A WordPress bridge plugin ships in the repo (integrations/wordpress/).

Feeds: local learning, shared recipes

Each site trains privately. When a new spam wave hits, signed feed channels (OpenWRT-style: Ed25519-signed JSON, pinned key, monotonic versions, expiry) deliver curated rules to every site. spamcha feed update in your cron.

Public engine, private intelligence

The engine is MIT and auditable: that's the point. What never goes public: your corpus, your trained model, your verdict log. Kerckhoffs said it first: the lock can sit in the shop window; the key and what your doorman learned cannot be copied.

En español: spamcha nace en inglés pero el español va incluido como locale de primera clase (--locale es). Documentación completa en el repositorio: README.es.md.
